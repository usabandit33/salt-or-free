A Charlotte water-treatment quote is not one machine. Name salt-based softening vs a salt-free conditioner before anyone cuts pipe.

Households mix ion-exchange softeners that use salt, salt-free conditioners that leave hardness in the water, and cheap no-salt gadgets sold as the same thing.

Ask which system is on the bid, whether hardness grains will drop on a test, and what salt or media you will buy later.

  • Ask whether the bid is a salt-based ion-exchange softener or a salt-free conditioner.
  • Ask whether a hardness test after install will show lower grains, or whether minerals stay in the water.
  • If it is a softener, ask sodium vs potassium salt, bag cost, and whether brine can go to a septic field.
  • Ask whether the quote assumes Charlotte city water or well water.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
